## The run

- **Probe:** one call per model on the OpenAI-compatible endpoint; both worked, so
  plan section 7's native-format fallback was not used. A re-probe after the
  reasoning-effort change is the committed `recordings/probes.json`.
- **Smoke:** 20 items x 2 models. Two recorded rounds (USD 0.121634 before the final
  caps, USD 0.130192 at them) plus an earlier exploratory round that left no rows.
  The first found the optional-key schema bug and the output caps; the last had 0
  invalid answers of 20 for both models.
- **Full run:** `make live`, run id `live`. The first attempt (2026-11-01T16:30Z)
  was killed by a host reboot after 69 primary cards; the resume
  (2026-11-02T01:02:46-02:14:59Z) skipped them and finished the remaining 331 primary
  and 200 comparison items. One paid call restored a response recording the reboot
  lost. The comparison ran the first 200 items after the section 8 cut.
- **Spend:** USD 1.584697 of USD 3 (primary USD 0.109051, comparison USD 1.475646).
  Even counting an unrecorded exploratory smoke at its worst case the total stays
  under about USD 1.89. No 403, abuse message or account warning appeared; no
  `reports/STOP.md` was written and no call was retried around a policy answer.
- **Headline metrics:** primary macro-F1 0.863 (0.825-0.895) on 400 items at USD
  0.244 per 1,000; comparison 0.851 (0.771-0.901) on 200 items at USD 6.125 per
  1,000. Final safety recall 1.000 for both, precision 0.359 / 0.487; quote grounding
  1.000; 0 invalid answers. The baselines score 0.415 macro-F1 and 0.887 / 0.568
  safety recall / precision.

## Deviations and shared-file changes

1. **`src/field_return_triage/extract/schema.py`** — R7's brief owns the lock,
   recordings, results and `reports/RUN.md`, not the schema. The change is the smoke
   fix: every field required, so the endpoint's JSON-schema mode enforces the keys
   the prompt asks for. Tests that exercised the old defaults still pass.
2. **`llm/client.py` and `llm/cost.py`** — committed earlier in this lane
   (`llm: cap reasoning effort, read the gateway list cost`): the reasoning-effort
   parameter for `muse-spark` and the gateway USD cost reader.
3. **Section 8 cut** — the comparison model ran 200 of 400 items; the guard makes
   the decision and `metrics.json` records `reduced: true`.
4. **Budget guard counts by key** — `read_calls` keys rows by (model, item), so a
   repeated paid call for one item counts once in the guard. The true spend is the
   raw row sum, and `reports/RUN.md` reports that figure against the cap.
5. **Resume gap on a reduced run** — after the one repair call, `make live` stopped
   at the budget guard when it re-estimated the comparison model's unscheduled half
   (items 201-400). The repair card was written before the check and the replay
   completed the run, but a future run that is reduced and then interrupted needs the
   cut persisted in the run state rather than re-derived from the cards.
6. **Reboot repair** — three files were NUL-padded by the crash; each was truncated
   to its last complete line. Nothing was reconstructed: the missing response was
   re-fetched with one paid call.
7. **Probe history** — the first probe round's rows were replaced when the probe was
   re-run; both rounds are counted in the spend table.
8. **HF metadata attempts in replay** — the embedding library makes a HEAD request
   for model metadata even when cached; offline it fails and falls back to the local
   cache. The replay is offline-safe but noisy; this is an R3/R6 follow-up.
